How to Reduce Passive Voice

Home / How to Reduce Passive Voice

To reduce passive voice, find each sentence that joins a form of be to a past participle, name the actor, and make that actor the subject. Keep the passive when the actor is unknown or when the action matters more. Rewrite one sentence at a time, then reread the whole paragraph.

Updated 2026-10-28

Step 1: find the passive sentences

The Passive Voice Converter looks for a form of be followed by a past participle: was written, is being updated, were made. It counts the sentences that match and reports the passive share against the active share.

The test is a pattern, not a parser. In a test of ten sentences, the converter flagged six. It missed one real passive, because an adverb sat between the verb and the participle: The cake was eaten quickly by the children. Read the flagged list, then read the text once yourself.

Step 2: name the actor

Ask who performs the action. If the answer is in the sentence, move it to the front:

  • The report was written by the team. becomes The team wrote the report.
  • The decision was made by the committee. becomes The committee made the decision.

If the actor is not in the sentence, the passive is hiding it. Name the actor, or say plainly that the actor is unknown.

Step 3: keep the passive when it earns its place

Some sentences work better passive. Keep it when the actor is unknown, when the action matters more than the actor, or when the actor is obvious: Our company was founded in 1995 needs no founder in the sentence. Mistakes were made is passive because nobody will name the person who made them, and that is the point of the sentence.

Rewrite, then measure

Change one sentence, then look at the ratio again. The ratio is the count of passive sentences divided by the count of all sentences, so a long paragraph dilutes a single passive sentence. Judge the ratio, not the count.

Paste the new version into the Passive Voice Converter. Each flagged sentence comes back with a suggested active rewrite, and the suggestion list is a checklist, not an answer: check that the actor it names is the real one.
