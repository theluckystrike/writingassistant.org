Reading Time by Word Count

Home / Reading Time by Word Count

The Word Counter on this site estimates reading time at 200 words per minute and speaking time at 130 words per minute. A 1,000-word text therefore takes 5.00 minutes to read silently and 7.69 minutes to read aloud, and the tool recalculates both figures as you type.

Updated 2026-10-28

How the estimate is calculated

The Word Counter divides the word count by a fixed rate. It shows the reading time label as Reading time (200 wpm) and the speaking time label as Speaking time (130 wpm). The division is words / 200 for reading and words / 130 for speaking, and the result is printed with two decimal places.

This is a flat rate per word. It does not model long words, tables, code blocks or numbers, which take longer to read than ordinary prose. Treat the result as an average, not as a stopwatch.

Which rate should you use

  • Use the 200 wpm figure when you write for a reader who skims a page or an email.
  • Use the 130 wpm figure when the words will be spoken: a talk, a video script, a podcast segment.
  • If your text will be read aloud, cut words until the speaking time fits the slot, not the reading time. The gap is large: 1,000 words is 5.00 minutes to read and 7.69 minutes to speak.
