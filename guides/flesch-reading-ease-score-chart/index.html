Flesch Reading Ease Score Chart

Home / Flesch Reading Ease Score Chart

A good Flesch Reading Ease score depends on the reader. This site's Readability Checker uses five bands: 80 or more is very easy, 60 to 79 is plain English, 40 to 59 is fairly difficult, 20 to 39 is difficult, and below 20 is very difficult. The FAQ on that page calls 60 to 70 plain English.

Updated 2026-10-28

The five score bands

These bands come from the audience function inside the Readability Checker. Each band needs a Flesch Reading Ease score at or above its floor:

Flesch Reading EaseBand the tool prints
80 and above5th grader / very easy
60 to 79Middle school / plain English
40 to 59High school / fairly difficult
20 to 39College / difficult
Below 20Professional / very difficult

The tool applies the same ladder to the Flesch-Kincaid grade level, so a text takes the easier of the two results. A page that scores 65 on Reading Ease lands in Middle school even if its grade level reads higher.

What the score measures

The score uses two averages only: words per sentence and syllables per word. The formula in the page is 206.835 - 1.015 x (words / sentences) - 84.6 x (syllables / words). Long sentences and long words both push the score down. Nothing else counts, so a text full of abstract short words can score well and still read badly.

Worked example

The default sample text in the Readability Checker is 58 words in 5 sentences with 107 syllables. Run through the same formula it gives:

MeasureValue
Words per sentence11.60
Syllables per word1.84
Flesch Reading Ease38.99
Flesch-Kincaid grade10.70
BandCollege / difficult

38.99 is a normal score for an explanation of a formula. The same text with shorter sentences would climb towards 50 without losing any meaning.

How to raise the score

  • Split sentences over 25 words. The tool lists them under the long-sentence heading.
  • Replace three-syllable words with shorter ones. The tool lists them as hard words.
  • Leave the numbers and names alone. They carry meaning, and shortening them does not help a reader.
